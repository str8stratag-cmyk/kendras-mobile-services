// 01 — CAPABILITIES

Two divisions. One standard.

Nursing that meets you at home, and a courier operation that treats a lab sample like mission cargo.

In-home clinical care

Licensed nursing delivered to homes, senior communities, and workplaces.

N-01

Medication Administration

Injections, IV meds, and medication management — administered and documented at home.

N-02

Wound Care

Dressing changes, wound assessment, and healing support — with full visit notes.

N-03

Clear updates to family and providers after every single visit.
