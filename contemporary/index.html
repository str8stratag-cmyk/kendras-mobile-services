What we do

Two services. One trusted team.

Whether you need skilled nursing at your kitchen table or a specimen across town before noon, we've got you covered.

Mobile Nursing

In-home care, hospital quality

Licensed nursing brought to homes, senior living communities, and workplaces.

Medication Administration

Injections, IV medications, and careful medication management in the comfort of home.

Wound Care

Dressing changes, wound assessment, and healing support with thorough documentation.

Chronic Care Monitoring

Blood pressure, glucose, and vital-sign checks — logged and reported to your provider.

Post-Surgical Care

Recovery checks, incision care, and coordination with your physician or surgical team.

Wellness Checks

Routine assessments, fall-risk screening, and straight-answer health education.

Care Coordination

About Kendra

A nurse who believes care should fit your life.

Kendra spent more than a decade in hospital nursing before building a practice that brings the same standard of care to people's doorsteps — for patients who can't, or shouldn't, make the trip.

The courier service grew from the same instinct: medical items deserve careful hands, clear communication, and someone who shows up on time.

  • Registered Nurse with 10+ years of clinical experience
  • Insured, bonded, and background-checked
  • Warm, unhurried visits — you're never just a number

"My goal is simple: healthcare should fit your life — not the other way around."

— Kendra, RN Founder & Lead Nurse
